In this updated 2026 guide, the Inframantra team has hand-picked the top 5 luxury apartments in Gurgaon — evaluating each project on price, location, RERA compliance, carpet area, builder reputation, possession timeline, and amenity quality. Why Gurgaon is the #1 Choice for Luxury Apartment Buyers in 2026

Before exploring individual projects, it is important to understand why luxury apartments in Gurgaon command such strong demand and consistent appreciation:

  • Corporate Hub: Home to Fortune 500 companies, Cyber City, and Udyog Vihar — driving sustained rental demand from CXO-level professionals
  • Capital Appreciation: Sectors on Golf Course Extension Road and Dwarka Expressway have delivered 108–150% price growth over the past decade
  • NRI Investment: RERA compliance, transparent documentation, and INR depreciation make Gurgaon luxury homes highly attractive for NRI investors
  • Rental Yields: Prime sectors near Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, and Dwarka Expressway offer 3–4% rental yields on luxury configurations

Frequently Asked Questions — Luxury Apartments in Gurgaon

Which is the poshest area for luxury apartments in Gurgaon?

Golf Course Road — particularly the stretch from Sector 42 to Sector 53 — is widely considered the most premium residential address in Gurgaon.

What is the price range of luxury apartments in Gurgaon in 2026?

Luxury apartments in Gurgaon in 2026 range from ₹4.50 Cr.* to ₹20 Cr+ for luxury configurations in projects like DLF Camellias or Trump Towers. On Dwarka Expressway, prices range from ₹14,000 to ₹22,500 per sq. ft., while Golf Course Road commands ₹26,000 per sq. ft. and above.

Are these luxury apartments RERA approved?

All 5 projects featured in this guide are either RERA registered or in the RERA approval process. Buyers are strongly advised to verify individual project RERA numbers on hrera.org.in before making any payment.

What is the best location to buy a luxury apartment in Gurgaon?

The best locations depend on your priority. For investment returns: Dwarka Expressway and SPR. For prestige address: Golf Course Road. For integrated township living: Southern Peripheral Road (Signature Global Cloverdale). For NRI buyers seeking established neighbourhoods: DLF Phases and Sohna Road.
