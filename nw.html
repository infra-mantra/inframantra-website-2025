Top 5 Most Expensive Societies in Gurgaon

1. TREVOC Royal Residences - One of the best societies in Gurgaon

Located on Golf Course Road, Trevoc Royal Residences is seen as the epitome of luxury and prestige. One of the most expensive societies in Gurgaon, TREVOC Royal Residences stands at a location that is conveniently connected to Golf Course Extension Road as well as the Southern Peripheral Road. Offering low density living, the property boasts only two high-rise towers, where only a limited number of families will stay. The layout is so spacious that both 3BHK and 4BHK come with master bedrooms, while the floor size of the apartment ranges from 2642 Sq.ft. to 3380 Sq.ft. Offering exclusivity at its best, this top residential society in Gurgaon features amenities such as a large swimming pool, health center, cafe, open theater, multipurpose hall, private dining zone, convenience store, and much more. With a multi-level security system, residents get access to five-level parking. All these are more, just for 172 families. Imagine the lifestyle you will get to relish everyday.

2. TARC Ishva - Address that’s as posh as home itself

TARC Ishva is yet another, most sought-after property on Golf Course Extension Road. Imagine your home close to the heart of an IT hub, sky-kissing buildings, glittering streets filled with hotels, restaurants, and cafes, and that’s what you get to access, if you get a home at TARC Ishva Sector 63A Gurgaon. What makes this place one of the best societies in Gurgaon is not one but multiple reasons. First is the location, Golf Course Road, which adds to the appeal of the property. Second is even better, which is the 7 acres of land parcel where 5 luxurious towers would stand. The property has more than 63 amenities to offer along with a larger than life clubhouse that’s approx. 1,15,000 sq.ft. Despite being developed in a large area, this one of the most expensive societies in Gurgaon will only be accessible to around 400 families, with amenities such as swimming pool, amphitheater, multipurpose hall, parking, multi-tier security, and even a dedicated health center.

3. Godrej Miraya- The trust of Godrej

The first attractive feature that would draw your attention is its location. Yes! Godrej Miraya is also located at Golf Course Road, Sector 43, very close to IFFCO Chowk. Imagine the convenience of seamless connectivity. This is yet another low density project where only 248 units are available for sale. This is not the only thing that makes it rise on the list of the top society in Gurgaon. The property offers luxurious 3 and 4BHK apartments with impressive floor size, which ranges from 2711 sq.ft. to 3897 sq.ft. The apartments have balconies on both sides, imagine the views that your eyes will feast on everyday. On top of that are the world-class amenities such as a swimming pool, multi-purpose hall, swimming pool, car-parking, senior citizen zone, and even restaurants.

4. BPTP Amaario

Would you like to get access to a five-star clubhouse for your whole life? Sounds so appealing right. This is just not the only reason why BPTP Amaario has its place among the best residential societies in Gurgaon. The society hosts a load of amenities which will only be available for its residents, such as a skywalk that’s 70-feet long, 7 high-speed lifts per tower, separate service lifts, fitness zone, beauty salon, yoga room, gym, kids play area, card room, swimming pool, amphitheater, and clubhouse that has spa inside. This property has only 4BHK apartments with an expansive floor size. Located at Dwarka Expressway, the property offers excellent connectivity, and accessibility to reputed schools, hospitals, and even workplaces.

5. DLF Privana West

Almost everyone in Gurgaon is familiar with the name DLF. The popularity of the brand has soured the popularity of DLF properties to new heights, and one of which is DLF Privana West. Located at Sector 76 Gurgaon, DLF Privana West offers excellent connectivity to Dwarka Expressway as well as Southern Peripheral Road. The property offers views of the majestique Aravalli mountains, while being a part of 116-acres township. The project itself spans over 15 acres of land, offering 85% open green spaces. Imagine living amid greenery for life. Moreover, DLF Privana only offers 4BHK spacious apartments that have 11.2 feet ceiling height with floor size ranging from 3577 sq.ft. to 5472 sq.ft. Reputed schools such as Delhi Public School, and Euro International School are also nearby.
